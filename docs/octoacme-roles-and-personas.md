# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create intuitive, user-centered experiences and advocate for end users throughout the product development lifecycle. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to ensure features are both valuable and usable.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs, behaviors, and pain points
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Conduct usability testing and gather feedback
- Collaborate with Product Managers during requirements gathering and refinement
- Partner with Developers during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Document design decisions and design systems

### Goals
- Deliver experiences that delight users and solve real problems
- Reduce friction and cognitive load in user workflows
- Ensure consistency and accessibility across features

### Interactions
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates during discovery and prioritization to understand user needs and validate solutions
- **With Developers**: Works closely during design handoff and implementation to ensure design intent is preserved
- **With QA**: Provides test scenarios focused on user experience and usability
- **With Stakeholders**: Presents user research findings and design rationale

### Typical Communication
- Design mockups and prototypes
- User research findings and insights
- Design review sessions with the team
- Accessibility and usability guidelines

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create clear, accessible documentation for features, processes, and releases. They ensure that complex information is translated into user-friendly guides, helping teams and customers understand how to use and operate the product.

### Responsibilities
- Write and maintain user-facing documentation (guides, tutorials, FAQs)
- Document processes and workflows for internal teams
- Create and update release notes with clear, concise messaging
- Collaborate with Developers and Product Managers to understand features
- Ensure consistency in terminology and tone across documentation
- Incorporate feedback and keep docs current with product changes

### Goals
- Reduce support burden by providing clear, self-service documentation
- Improve user onboarding and product adoption
- Ensure documentation is accessible and easy to navigate

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Gathers technical details, code samples, and implementation specifics
- **With Product Managers**: Understands feature intent, use cases, and target audience
- **With Support Engineers**: Collects common questions and pain points to address in docs
- **With QA**: Validates documentation accuracy against implemented features

### Typical Communication
- Draft documentation and style guides
- Release notes and migration guides
- Documentation audits and gap analysis

---

## QA Lead / Tester

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Testers define and execute comprehensive test strategies, validate features against acceptance criteria, and escalate critical issues. They ensure quality standards are met before release and help prevent defects from reaching users.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and plans for features and releases
- Execute manual and automated testing across test environments
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Identify, document, and prioritize bugs and quality issues
- Collaborate with Developers on root cause analysis and fixes
- Prepare and execute smoke tests before production releases
- Track quality metrics and trends

### Goals
- Catch defects early and prevent critical issues in production
- Ensure consistent quality across all releases
- Build confidence in product reliability and performance

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Communicates issues, collaborates on test coverage, validates fixes
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria and provides quality feedback
- **With Project Managers**: Reports blockers and ensures release readiness
- **With Support Engineers**: Gathers field issues and edge cases to incorporate into testing

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Bug reports with reproduction steps
- Quality metrics and coverage reports
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Support Engineer / Customer Advocate

### Role Summary
Support Engineers serve as the voice of the customer, handling support requests, triaging issues, and identifying patterns in customer pain points. They bridge the gap between customers and the product team, ensuring customer needs inform product development.

### Responsibilities
- Triage and resolve customer support requests
- Document and categorize customer issues and feedback
- Escalate critical or recurring issues to the product team
- Identify trends in customer pain points and feature requests
- Communicate product updates and workarounds to customers
- Provide training and guidance to help customers succeed
- Gather field data to inform product decisions

### Goals
- Deliver exceptional customer support and satisfaction
- Identify improvement opportunities grounded in real customer needs
- Reduce customer friction and support burden

### Interactions
- **With Product Managers**: Provides customer feedback, usage patterns, and feature request trends
- **With Developers**: Communicates workarounds and helps triage technical issues
- **With Project Managers**: Alerts to customer-impacting risks and escalations
- **With QA**: Reports field issues and edge cases discovered in production

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Feature request documentation with customer context
- Support escalation and incident reports

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Consider cross-functional collaboration points when planning projects to ensure all roles are engaged appropriately.
